# About

Renaissance Labs is a small group working on language technology, mostly for Swedish, and on explaining hard ideas clearly. This wiki is where we keep what we try, what worked, and what did not.

Most pages are working notes rather than polished articles. Where a number comes from our own small test, the page says so, and where it comes from somewhere else we link the source.

## What we work on

- **Speech and language models for Swedish:** text-to-speech, speech recognition, and evaluation.
- **Explainer films:** videos rendered entirely from code, on AI, hardware and psychology.
- **Evaluation:** benchmarks that measure things existing leaderboards miss, such as how natural written Swedish sounds.
